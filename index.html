The built environment, time use, and subjective wellbeing

I am an Isaac Newton Trust Fellow and Senior Teaching Associate in the Department of Land Economy at the University of Cambridge. My research asks how the places people live in, and the way they spend their time, shape how they feel, using longitudinal survey, time-use, and health-record data with causal inference and machine learning methods.

Fellow of Queens’ College · Director of Studies at St Edmund’s College · Fellow of the Higher Education Academy · ONS Accredited Researcher
